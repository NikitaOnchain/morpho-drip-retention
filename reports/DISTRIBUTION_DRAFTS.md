**What remains after lending incentives end?**

I studied Morpho on Arbitrum using a fixed panel of 45 DRIP-eligible markets and reproducible onchain state reconstruction. By +180, the baseline-adjusted campaign-end debt uplift retained 7.7% for USDC and 11.3% for USD₮0, while material borrower-count uplift retained 63.8%.

The distinction matters: outstanding balances, new borrow flows, interest accrual and borrower participation are different measures. The analysis does not identify a causal DRIP effect or verified reward recipients.

For a future program, I would test narrower market selection and staged rewards against precommitted balance and material-borrower persistence targets. The project includes exact definitions, code, figures, QA and explicit limitations.
