# Reports

The Phase 8 release candidate is [A Narrower Incentive Test for Morpho](DRIP_LENDING_DEMAND_REPORT.md), a short decision memo complementing the answer-first [root README](../README.md). [Distribution drafts](DISTRIBUTION_DRAFTS.md) are unsent and retain pseudonym/URL placeholders. Independent review and publication approval are still pending.

Public-facing analysis belongs here and should be written in English.

The final report should contain:

1. the decision question;
2. campaign and protocol context;
3. metric and cohort definitions;
4. data sources and QA;
5. three to six charts;
6. findings with uncertainty;
7. alternative explanations and limitations;
8. a practical recommendation for incentive design.

Do not publish campaign-period growth as durable retention before the post-incentive checks pass.

`PHASE7_FINDINGS.md` is the reproducible English findings note for the focused
question about post-incentive lending-demand persistence. It reports exact
token groups separately, distinguishes debt balances from flows and interest,
includes the six accepted charts, states alternative explanations, and makes
no causal DRIP claim.
